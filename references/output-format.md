# Output format

Use this order for a full report.

## 1. Research Context

Question, scope, live or replay mode, as-of time, cutoff, currency, and access boundary.

## 2. Executive Finding

One sentence with classification, confidence, strongest support, largest unknown, and invalidation condition.

## 3. System Shift

Observed change, mechanism, affected process, timing, and evidence.

## 4. Dependency Map

Inputs, process steps, outputs, customers, alternatives, and each material edge.

## 5. Constraint Assessment

Constraint dimensions, supply state, evidence quality, qualification friction, ramp latency, yield, merchant availability, and route-around difficulty.

## 6. Company / Issuer Exposure

Role, product or service, direct exposure, customer path, geography, and exposure confidence.

## 7. Capture Path

Pricing, retention, scarcity, switching cost, allocation power, ownership, and leakage to competitors or customers.

## 8. Financial & Capital Bridge

Physical driver, volume, price, mix, yield, utilization, revenue, gross profit, operating cash flow, capex, funding, dilution, and per-share effect. Show which links are measured and which are assumptions.

## 9. Market Expectation Gap

Price and market value with dates and arithmetic, valuation basis, observable expectations, scenario ranges, and the specific variant view.

## 10. Countercase

Local, material, and fatal failure modes. Include contradictory evidence rather than only generic risks.

## 11. Disproof Plan

For every material or fatal countercase: target claim, test, signal, threshold, time window, owner, and action.

## 12. Unknowns & Next Checks

Unknowns ranked by decision impact, information value, cost, and next source or experiment.

## 13. Evidence Ledger

Claim ID, evidence ID, source type, locator, published_at, known_at, retrieved_at, access, independence group, epistemic label, status, and notes.

## Confidence rubric

- High: all critical gates pass and independent evidence covers the causal chain.
- Medium: the chain is plausible but one material link is partial or expectation data is weak.
- Low: the map is useful but necessity, capture, or timing remains unresolved.
