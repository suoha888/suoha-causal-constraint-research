# Research dialogue

The dialogue is a control surface for research quality. It is not a script for producing a predetermined conclusion.

## Intake questions

Ask these questions before deep collection:

1. What exact decision or belief should the research resolve?
2. Is this a live view or a historical replay? What is the cutoff timestamp?
3. What system, process, customer behavior, or policy changed?
4. Which physical or economic dependency might become binding?
5. Which issuer, instrument, or route is in scope?
6. What would count as a disconfirming observation?
7. Which sources are public, restricted, private, or synthetic?
8. What output is needed: map, case, update, comparison, or decision memo?

If the user cannot answer all questions, record the missing fields as UNKNOWN and continue only with a bounded map.

## Investigation prompts

For each material claim, ask:

- What is the claim in one sentence?
- Is it a fact, inference, hypothesis, unknown, or contradiction?
- What is the earliest date it was knowable?
- What source directly supports it?
- Is the source independent of the issuer or thesis author?
- What alternative explanation or route could produce the same observation?
- Which supply state is actually observed?
- What must be true for the edge to matter economically?
- Who captures the value, and why can that capture persist?
- What is the next broken link in the financial bridge?
- What observation would downgrade or invalidate the claim?

## Decision language

Use conditional language:

- “The case is supported if…”
- “This edge is not established because…”
- “The evidence is consistent with…, but does not prove…”
- “Promotion requires…”
- “The case is invalidated if…”

Avoid certainty words unless the evidence and gate state justify them. Never convert a source’s marketing language into a verified capacity or customer commitment.

## Final questions

Before publishing, ask:

- Could another analyst reproduce the evidence ledger?
- Did any post-cutoff information leak into the replay?
- Did exposure get mistaken for capture?
- Did nameplate capacity get mistaken for available qualified supply?
- Did the market value arithmetic reconcile?
- Is the countercase serious enough to change the position?
- Is every material uncertainty visible in the executive finding?
