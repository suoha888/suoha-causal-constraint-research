# Customer validation kernel

Customer evidence is an independent rung, not a synonym for issuer exposure. Keep qualification, design win, purchase order, repeat order, long-term agreement, prepayment, capacity reservation, take-or-pay, second-source qualification, cancellation, and delay as separate signal types.

Supplier self-reporting may establish `ISSUER_REPORTED`; it does not automatically establish an independently confirmed customer relationship. A design win does not automatically establish revenue, and a purchase order does not automatically establish repeat demand.

The customer gate requires usable CUSTOMER, REGULATOR or INDEPENDENT_TECHNICAL evidence (SYNTHETIC only in fixtures), an appropriate positive signal and resolved customer identity. Issuer self-report alone cannot pass. Supported cancellation, delay or second-source signals prevent automatic positive promotion until their scope and consequences are reviewed; evidence of an adverse event remains a supported fact, not positive demand validation. Signal polarity is distinct from source validity.
