# Market reflexivity kernel

Price and volume are observations about the market, not automatic validation of a physical constraint. Record whether publicity preceded the move, the liquidity state, volume discontinuity, social attention, and independent fundamental confirmation.

Hard rules:

- price movement alone cannot pass constraint validation;
- volume spikes alone cannot pass customer validation;
- social attention cannot establish economic capture;
- a low-liquidity move after publication is non-independent market evidence until separately confirmed.
