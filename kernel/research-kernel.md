# Research kernel

The kernel is the smallest reasoning loop that every full investigation must satisfy:

    question -> system shift -> dependency -> constraint -> edge
    -> issuer exposure -> capture -> capital bridge -> expectation
    -> countercase -> disproof

## Promotion rules

Promote a system shift only when its timing and mechanism are supported.

Promote a constraint only when necessity, friction, and the relevant supply state are separately evidenced.

Promote an edge only when the relationship and direction are explicit. A company mention is not an edge.

Promote exposure only when the issuer role, product or service, and dependency path are evidenced.

Promote capture only when the issuer can retain part of the value after customer bargaining, substitution, competition, and capacity response.

Promote an operating case only when the physical driver reaches at least one measured financial link.

Promote a variant case only when the market expectation and the research expectation are both dated and comparable.

Promote to research-ready only when all gates pass and a disproof plan can change the conclusion.

## Negative evidence

Absence of disclosure is not proof of absence, but repeated disclosure of alternatives, low utilization, customer concentration, price pressure, or dilution is evidence against a strong case. Negative evidence receives its own ledger record.
