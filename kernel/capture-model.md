# Capture model

Exposure answers whether the issuer participates in the dependency. Capture answers whether it retains economics.

Score each capture path from 0 to 5 and explain the score:

0. no verified exposure;
1. indirect or easily substituted exposure;
2. exposure with limited differentiation;
3. differentiated position with partial pricing or retention evidence;
4. strong position with repeatability, qualification, or allocation evidence;
5. durable control of a scarce, hard-to-replace value pool with observable economics.

The score is a summary, not a substitute for evidence. Test:

- who pays;
- what is scarce;
- what can be substituted;
- how long qualification takes;
- who owns the customer relationship;
- whether price, mix, yield, or utilization improves;
- whether competition or customer vertical integration can take the value;
- whether capex and dilution absorb the value.

Always show leakage from system value to issuer value.
