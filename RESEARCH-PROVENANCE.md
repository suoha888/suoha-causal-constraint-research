# Research provenance and data boundary

## Public project layer

This is an independently authored research-software and methodology project. The public repository contains project-authored terminology, schemas, contracts, code, validators, documentation, source-adapter interfaces, and synthetic fixtures.

The public layer is designed to be understandable and reusable without requiring access to a private archive. Public factual outputs should carry reproducible evidence from sources that can be cited and legally used for that output.

## Private research-input layer

The maintainer may separately maintain a private research corpus for methodology development, historical replay, error analysis, and internal evaluation. The corpus may include records collected, organized, and indexed by the maintainer, including material subject to third-party access conditions, subscription terms, copyright, database rights, privacy rules, or other restrictions.

The private corpus is not distributed by this repository. It is not included in the repository license. The existence of a local copy, index, metadata record, or hash does not assert ownership of the underlying third-party work or a right to redistribute it.

## Method abstraction

General research questions, factual relationships, analytical procedures, and system-design ideas may inform the project. The public implementation is independently expressed through project-authored terminology, data models, code, tests, and documentation. The project does not intentionally reproduce third-party source prose, images, screenshots, subscription material, private datasets, or other protected expression.

## Public claims

Restricted or private material must not be silently converted into a public fact. If a conclusion cannot be independently established from publishable evidence, the public state remains `UNKNOWN`, `PARTIAL`, or `NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

Public accessibility, factual relevance, citation, and redistribution rights are separate concepts. This repository does not assume that viewable material is freely redistributable or that subscription access creates a right to republish it.

## Scope of this notice

This document describes an engineering and publication boundary; it is not a legal opinion. Specific source terms, database rights, privacy issues, licensing, cross-jurisdiction use, and substantial-similarity questions should be reviewed by qualified counsel when material to a release.
